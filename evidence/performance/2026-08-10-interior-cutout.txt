DECISION

Use the shader volume as production default. The repeatable ~0.38 ms cost in the
handover's worst-case close exterior framing is accepted for depth correctness
and a reusable hull-volume mechanism. Keep the stencil selectable as a fallback
if a future GPU budget needs that time back.
