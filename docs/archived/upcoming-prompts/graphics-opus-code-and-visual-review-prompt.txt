TECHNICAL CODE REVIEW

Review at minimum:

1. COLOUR AND POST-PROCESSING PIPELINE

- Lighting/shader work occurs in an appropriate linear space.
- Output colour conversion and tone mapping occur exactly once.
- Colour, data and normal textures use correct colour-space treatment.
- Exposure and bloom use intended HDR values without lifting ordinary clouds,
  ocean or the complete frame.

2. ASTRONOMICAL AUTHORITY AND CONTINUITY

- Sun, Moon, star and atmosphere inputs derive from canonical planetary state.
- No clock-label shortcut or second decorative celestial authority exists.
- Atmosphere, exposure, star visibility, Moonlight and lamp thresholds are
  finite and continuous through horizon, twilight, polar day and polar night.
- Smoothing uses presentation delta rather than accelerated world time.

3. SHADER AND LIGHTING CORRECTNESS

- Radiance, Fresnel, glitter, foam and material-lighting terms have coherent
  units/ranges and bounded edge cases.
- Star limiting magnitude is monotonic and brighter stars cannot appear later
  than fainter ones solely because of the visibility function.
- Lamp hysteresis and forced modes are deterministic.
- Shader variants, uniforms, disposal and resize paths cannot leak resources or
  leave stale state.

4. PREREQUISITE PRESERVATION

- Graphics changes do not alter `WaveField` displacement, CPU/GPU parity,
  sea-state transitions, persistent-foam history or `RaftBuoyancy`.
- Close/default/far/embodied camera ownership and transitions remain intact.
- No second observer, camera, time, world-position or ocean-physics authority is
  introduced.

5. TOOLS, TESTS AND PERFORMANCE

- One shared launcher owns World/General, Buoyancy, Ocean, Camera and Graphics;
  deep links open the same state, closed panels are lazy, and `Hide all` is safe.
- Tests cover numerical boundaries and continuity rather than only nominal
  screenshots.
- Browser/shader console evidence is clean.
- 4K, far-camera, star-heavy night and mobile performance claims are measured.
- New assets are licensed and credited.

For every technical finding, cite the exact file and tight line range, explain
the concrete failure mode, identify missing test coverage, and provide a narrow
acceptance condition. Do not provide a patch.

JUDGING CRITERIA

Judge:

- clear sky hue and zenith-to-horizon gradient;
- cloud whiteness, internal shading and compositing;
- horizon readability without a seam or broad grey veil;
- deep-open-ocean body colour;
- view-dependent, spatially localised Sun glitter;
- roughness-dependent glint across calm/moderate/rough seas;
- preservation and plausible lighting of generated/persistent foam;
- direct-Sun versus ambient-light balance;
- warm low-Sun sail response and restrained backlighting;
- continuous geographically coherent sunrise/sunset/twilight;
- magnitude-ordered star emergence and crisp 4K star points;
- dark but readable nights;
- restrained Moon halo and directionally correct Moonlight/reflection;
- small warm raft-lamp refuge without ocean floodlighting;
- stable exposure without pumping;
- clipping, banding, gamut errors, double-gamma symptoms, oversaturation or
  uncontrolled bloom;
- consistency across every final camera scale and viewport;
- discoverable tools and a genuinely clean `Hide all` state.

Distinguish physical incoherence from aesthetic preference. Do not request a
weather system, new camera mode, new sea-state physics or unrelated content.
