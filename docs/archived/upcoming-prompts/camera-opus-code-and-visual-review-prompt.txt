TECHNICAL CODE REVIEW

Review at minimum:

1. STATE AND COORDINATE AUTHORITY

- Camera state remains local presentation state.
- Camera input, transitions and altitude never mutate ECEF position/velocity,
  UTC, latitude/longitude, transported frame or accelerated world time.
- No planar travel or renderer-recentering model has been introduced.

2. OWNERSHIP AND LIFECYCLE

- Active camera, mode, input and transition ownership are unambiguous.
- Mode switches cannot leave duplicate listeners, controls, callbacks or stale
  state.
- Resize, orientation change, disposal and reinitialisation paths are complete.
- Cinematic and embodied state restoration is deterministic.

3. NUMERICAL AND INTERACTION BEHAVIOUR

- Scale curves, altitude, distance, FOV, optical pitch and framing targets are
  finite, bounded, continuous and intentionally separated.
- Damping and transitions use presentation delta and remain frame-rate
  independent.
- Near-vertical embodied look avoids singularities, yaw inversion and NaNs.
- Pointer, wheel, touch and pinch ownership is scoped correctly to the canvas.
- Transition-time input has one consistent policy.

4. OCEAN, BUOYANCY AND AUDIO PRESERVATION

- Far-camera LOD/filtering changes presentation rather than physical wave
  displacement or raft sampling.
- Sea-state transitions, persistent foam and `RaftBuoyancy` remain intact.
- The existing non-positional `Ambience` architecture remains valid.
- No `AudioListener` is invented unnecessarily; if one exists, ownership is
  singular and correct.

5. TOOLS, TESTS AND PERFORMANCE

- The shared launcher exposes World/General, Buoyancy, Ocean and Camera through
  one state model, with lazy closed panels and safe `Hide all`.
- Tests cover curve boundaries, mode ownership, transition completion, input,
  vertical look, world-state isolation, serialisation and resize.
- Far-view topology, clipping and performance claims have measured evidence.
- No avoidable per-frame allocations or work proportional to unsupported ocean
  extent has been introduced.

For every technical finding, cite an exact file and tight line range, explain
the concrete failure, identify the missing or insufficient test, and provide a
narrow acceptance condition. Do not provide a patch.

JUDGING CRITERIA

Explicitly answer:

- Does zooming out reveal substantially more ocean rather than only shrinking
  the raft?
- Does altitude increase meaningfully?
- Does the raft become tiny without an icon, billboard or artificial scaling?
- Is there a strong default composition at close, default, medium and far
  scales?
- Are camera position, optical pitch and target/framing decoupled well?
- Is the horizon stable and free of water-edge or topology failures?
- Do distant waves, foam and spray filter plausibly without shimmer or giant
  white tiles?
- Does the embodied camera feel located at the castaway's eye position?
- Can it look through 360 degrees, directly up and meaningfully down?
- Does the sail occlude naturally, and does lowering it clear the view?
- Are motion and head stabilisation connected but tolerable?
- Do transitions avoid clipping, spins and disorienting paths?
- Does returning restore the user's prior cinematic state?
- Are controls legible on desktop and touch?
- Are all debug panels discoverable, and does `Hide all` restore a clean view?

SEVERITY

  transitions are unsafe/unusable, or canonical experience is invalid.
- MAJOR: recurring composition, motion, clipping, LOD or usability defect.
- MINOR: polish issue suitable for documentation or the graphics round.
