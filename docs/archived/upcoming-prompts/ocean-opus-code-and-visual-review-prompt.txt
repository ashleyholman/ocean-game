visual critic for a candidate implementation of the ocean sea-state and
whitewater round in an existing Three.js raft prototype.

The original Codex implementation session is the sole code integrator. Your job
is to identify code, architecture, numerical, test, performance and visual
defects precisely enough that a follow-up implementation session can reproduce,
prioritise and fix them.

Do not anchor on another reviewer’s conclusions before forming your own. The
value of this review is an independent model’s reading of both code and output.

TECHNICAL CODE REVIEW

Review at minimum:

1. WORLD AND COORDINATE AUTHORITY

- `PlanetaryWorld` remains the only geographic travel/time authority.
- The renderer remains raft-centred and no planar travel or recentering model
  has returned.
- `WaveField.setOrigin()` remains a presentation-phase diagnostic rather than a
  second geographic position.
- True/local-tangent wind and swell directions use one documented convention.

2. WAVE MODEL AND NUMERICAL CONTRACT

- Sea-state component generation is deterministic for seed and parameters.
- Wind sea, primary swell and secondary swell are genuinely independent systems.
- CPU and GPU consume the same components, phase, time, derivatives and
  transition state.
- Gerstner inverse displacement, Jacobian/normal composition and orbital
  velocity remain coherent and finite.
- Parameter transitions do not regenerate phases, snap headings or introduce
  energy discontinuities.
- Extreme inputs are bounded or classified without hiding NaNs or mesh failure.

3. WHITEWATER AND RESOURCE LIFECYCLE

- Whitecap generation uses defensible surface/wind indicators.
- Persistent foam has explicit generation, advection, decay and breakup state.
- Presentation-origin and texture wrapping are continuous.
- Render targets, ping-pong state, resize, quality changes and disposal are
  correct and bounded.
- Spray and raft-contact effects remain separate, with no unbounded allocations.

4. BUOYANCY AND EXISTING-SYSTEM PRESERVATION

- The 24-station, 240 Hz force-based buoyancy contract remains intact.
- No preset-specific raft constants or rendering-driven flotation fixes exist.
- Raft-survivable and extreme classifications match actual regression evidence.
- `?debug=buoyancy`, its parity tools and existing world/astronomy behaviour
  remain functional.

5. DEVELOPER TOOLS, TESTS AND PERFORMANCE

- One visible launcher owns World/General, Buoyancy and Ocean panels.
- Query parameters open the same state as discoverable buttons.
- Closed heavy labs are lazy and `Hide all` does not reset simulation state.
- Tests exercise determinism, CPU/GPU parity, transitions, wrapping, frame-rate
  independence and failure boundaries rather than only happy-path presets.
- Performance and memory claims are measured and supported by the implementation.

For every technical finding, cite the exact file and tight line range, explain
the concrete failure mode, identify the missing or insufficient test, and give a
narrow acceptance condition. Do not supply a replacement implementation.

JUDGING CRITERIA

Judge whether:

- each preset is recognisably different in period, scale, direction, spread,
  grouping, roughness and whitewater—not one spectrum with amplitude changed;
- remote swell remains visually independent from local wind;
- crossing seas contain two coherent, legible systems rather than arbitrary
  noise;
- calm water is genuinely calm and glassy swell is broad and organised;
- rough seas convey large-scale energy without merely moving faster;
- crests propagate coherently and avoid obvious sinusoidal repetition;
- whitecaps arise for plausible steepness/wind reasons;
- persistent foam survives, advects, fragments and decays;
- foam does not remain painted rigidly on crests;
- foam and detail remain continuous at diagnostic presentation-origin and wrap
  boundaries;
- spindrift is directional, sparse and scale-appropriate rather than snow,
  smoke or fog blobs;
- raft-contact spray remains distinct from ocean whitewater;
- raft and rendered water appear to agree;
- LOD transitions, horizon views and mobile/4K presentation remain credible;
- the tools launcher is discoverable and `Hide all` produces a clean view.

Do not fail this ocean round for missing final graphics-round features such as
new atmosphere, colour grading, material overhaul or the future multi-scale
camera. Judge the water within the integrated current visual style.

SEVERITY

- BLOCKER: invalidates the round, hides a broken physical contract, prevents
  required evidence, or makes a major preset unusable.
- MAJOR: conspicuous recurring defect that materially harms physical
  intelligibility or visual quality.
- MINOR: local polish issue that can be deferred without misrepresenting the
  result.
