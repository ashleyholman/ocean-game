REVIEW AREAS

1. Colour pipeline

- Linear/display colour spaces are explicit and transformed exactly once.
- Colour, data and normal textures are tagged appropriately.
- Tone mapping, exposure and post-processing do not double-transform values.
- Bloom operates on intended HDR sources and cannot lift broad ordinary areas.

2. Astronomical authority

- Sun, Moon and star directions derive only from canonical planetary state.
- Atmosphere and lighting use Sun elevation/direction, not clock-label
  shortcuts.
- No second time-of-day, observer, decorative Sun/Moon or star authority exists.
- Polar day/night and horizon edge cases remain finite.

3. Continuity and numerical safety

- Atmosphere, exposure, star visibility, lamp thresholds and light intensities
  are finite, bounded and continuous.
- Smoothing uses presentation delta, never accelerated world time.
- Star magnitude ordering is monotonic.
- Lamp hysteresis prevents threshold chatter.
- Shader maths avoids NaN, negative radiance where invalid, divide-by-zero and
  precision failures.

4. Ocean optics and prerequisite preservation

- Body radiance, Fresnel reflection, direct glitter and foam lighting are
  distinct terms with defensible ranges.
- Roughness alters glint distribution without changing physical displacement.
- Final sea-state presets, transition logic, persistent foam history,
  `WaveField` CPU/GPU parity and `RaftBuoyancy` remain intact.
- Graphics code does not replace whitewater with a static mask or introduce a
  new wave model.

5. Camera preservation

- Close/default/far/embodied modes and transitions remain intact.
- Graphics changes do not create a second camera or observer state.
- Far-camera paths use bounded work and appropriate LOD.

6. Developer tools

- World/General, Buoyancy, Ocean, Camera and Graphics are reachable from one
  visible launcher.
- Query parameters open the same shared panels as deep links.
- `Hide all` does not mutate canonical, camera, ocean, foam or exposure state.
- Closed heavy panels are not eagerly constructed.

7. Performance and resources

- No per-frame avoidable allocations or per-star CPU loops without
  justification.
- Render-target sizes, shader variants, bloom and post-processing are bounded.
- 4K, far-camera, night-star and mobile claims have measured evidence.
- New assets are licensed and credited.

8. Tests and documentation

- All pre-existing tracked planetary, raft-water, ocean, parity, lab and camera
  tests remain.
- Tests cover atmosphere boundaries, exposure, stars, lamp hysteresis, optics,
  world-state isolation and shader/browser failure paths.
- Reports describe actual values and measured results rather than intentions.
