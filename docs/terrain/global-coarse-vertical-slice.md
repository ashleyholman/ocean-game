# Global coarse terrain vertical slice

Status: implemented and correctness-tested through TERR-G006 on 2026-08-18. This is an
authorised integration slice, not Gate A's production global terrain build and
not evidence that R2 has begun.

## Outcome

One immutable coarse source now answers every coarse-global question the live
game asks:

- `DeveloperGlobe` samples it for recognisable land and broad relief; and
- `TerrainSystem` requests a local 3 × 3 tile neighbourhood from it at the
  canonical vessel position, including immediately after a World-panel
  teleport; and
- the voyage governor receives a live nearest-land distance from the same
  decoded Natural Earth rings.

There is no second mutable latitude/longitude state. `TerrainSystem.update()`
converts the canonical ECEF position through the existing WGS84 boundary,
compares the provider's stable location key, and rebuilds resident geometry
only when that key changes. The existing geometry-only `TerrainTileGeometry`
contract and public `loadTiles()` path remain intact for the synthetic R1
fixtures and later higher-resolution providers.

Runtime selection is:

- `?terrain=global`: this coarse global source;
- absent `terrain` or `?terrain=synthetic`: the unchanged R1
  fixture/range/bearing harness; and
- `?terrain=off`: no terrain, for open-water baselines.

The global slice remains intentionally explicit. The canonical authored
opening at the time was a city centre on land, which the real mask correctly
classified as such (the opening has since moved to open water in the Tasman
Sea; the land case survives as an explicit test fixture). Only `?terrain=global` now runs the accepted
water-aware resolver before constructing `PlanetaryWorld`; synthetic and off
startup preserve the authored coordinates exactly and do not load this path.
This makes explicit global mode usable without silently changing the shipping
default or the authored opening constants.

## Coast-distance and voyage-governor contract

The decoded land rings compile once into 5,004 non-degenerate spherical coast
segments. `nearestLandM(latitude, longitude)` returns exact zero wherever the
same source classifies land; over ocean it performs one fixed complete scan and
returns the shortest spherical point-to-segment arc, converted with the mean
WGS84 radius. There is no second raster, land mask, expanding search, or
data-dependent query bound.
The manifest pins the role, algorithm version, segment count, radius policy,
land-zero policy, and non-navigational purpose.

The source handles longitude wrap in the same normalisation path as sampling,
chooses the existing longitude-zero gauge at exact poles, and rejects invalid
coordinates. `GlobalTerrainHandle.update()` derives geodetic position from the
latest canonical ECEF state, refreshes the distance only when that position
changes, and caches the scalar for `getNearestLandM()`. In `main.ts`, that update
runs before the voyage-clock hook consumes the value.

At the tested 3 m/s and 0.2°/s governor settings, central Pacific clearance
allows the 30× ceiling, a near-coast ocean point about 0.86 km from the
coarse coast slows below 30×, and a land point reports zero and therefore 1×.
This is a presentation-rate query over generalised Natural Earth geometry, not
a navigational clearance, collision, or grounding authority.

## Temporary tile profile

The provider uses a deliberately replaceable geographic lattice:

- 2° tiles;
- 33 × 33 samples (`2^5 + 1`);
- a 3 × 3 neighbourhood away from the poles;
- exact global integer cell coordinates for shared samples;
- unwrapped cell indices across the antimeridian, so both sides of a resident
  seam use bit-identical height and normal inputs;
- a finite, clamped two-row neighbourhood at an exact pole; and
- −80 m visual sea floor where the coarse mask says water.

This addressing does not leak into `TerrainSystem` or the mesh builder. A
future cube-sphere and real-data provider can replace it behind
`TerrainTileProvider`.

## Water-aware opening resolver — TERR-G006 complete

`WaterAwareOpeningResolver` is a pure deterministic query over the same
`GlobalTerrainSource.sample()` and `nearestLandM()` authority. An authored
point already on water with the required clearance is copied without
normalisation or recomputation, including signed-zero identity. Otherwise it
checks a finite lattice of geodesic rings in ascending displacement. Bearings
are ordered from the authored outbound course, with clockwise winning an exact
angular tie. The first accepted point is therefore the minimum-displacement
qualified candidate in the stated lattice, not a claim of continuous geometric
optimality.

The default profile is 5 km minimum coast clearance, 500 m radial steps, 5°
bearing steps and a 100 km maximum radius: at most 14,401 candidates including
the authored point. Invalid coordinates or profiles throw before search;
exhaustion returns structured failure and explicit-global bootstrap turns that
into a startup error.

Against the pinned Natural Earth source, the land opening of the time resolved
reproducibly on ring 49 after 3,461 candidates: authored city centre (`land`,
reason `authored_land`) to `-35.02485°, 138.35887°`, 24.5 km away with
5,011.85 m coarse-source clearance. This is derived, not a hard-coded Gulf
coordinate. The immutable authored/resolved/reason/source record remains
visible in both player and developer World panels after startup.

Exact-pole searches use an explicit local-tangent gauge instead of depending
on `atan2(0, 0)`: at the north pole the finite-latitude north basis continues
on the opposite meridian; at the south pole it follows the authored meridian.

## Water-aware globe selections — TERR-G006 follow-up complete

Both player and developer World panels now qualify explicit-global globe
clicks through the same resolver and Natural Earth source. A click already on
water with at least 5 km coarse clearance is copied bit-identically. A land or
near-coast click uses a documented true-north tie gauge, then the same 500 m ×
5° bounded lattice; a successful result teleports to qualified coarse water
and shows the displacement beside the globe marker. Exhausting every lattice
candidate through the 100 km ring rejects the click, keeps canonical world
position unchanged, and reports lattice exhaustion beside the marker. Feedback
is anchored to that outcome and clears after more than 10 m of ordinary travel.
The named sea shortcuts remain authored open-water destinations and clear any
previous click feedback.

A live in-app-browser pass exercised the main visible outcomes: an Alice
Springs click exhausted the 100 km lattice, a Port Lincoln click moved 15.0 km
to qualified coarse water, and the marker/caption followed the canonical
world. This is deliberately an occasional synchronous UI query, not route
validation or a per-frame navigation service.

## Explicit limitations and staged work

This slice must not be used as evidence for TERR-200 through TERR-227, Gate A,
or P1 completion.

- Natural Earth 1:110m land is intentionally generalised. It is suitable for a
  small globe and farthest fallback, not a coastline approached by a vessel.
- Relief comes from 19 sparse extrema and a visual envelope, not continuous
  measured elevation.
- Inland water classes are not represented separately.
- Geographic tiles become anisotropic at high latitude. Cube-sphere selection
  remains a Gate A decision.
- There is no parent/child LOD, geomorph, streaming, disk cache, route prefetch,
  local override, collision, grounding or navigational query.
- The nearest-coast result inherits Natural Earth's 1:110m generalisation. It
  is suitable for the voyage presentation governor, not navigation or safety.
- The default 25 km camera far plane still limits what local coarse geometry
  can show. This slice does not bypass the unresolved R1 depth/ocean gates.
- Explicit-global startup and World-panel globe clicks cannot place the vessel
  on this source's land mask. Other programmatic teleports are unchanged.
- Water-aware selection is not collision, grounding, route validation or
  navigational clearance; a moving vessel can still cross coarse land.
- Shipping still defaults to `synthetic`. TERR-G006 proves a deterministic
  coarse-source start, but does not by itself approve global as the default.

Most importantly, **Copernicus DEM GLO-30 2024_1 and its Water Body Mask have
not been acquired or ingested**. Their manifest entries remain
`not_acquired`, and the canonical ingestion pipeline remains `not_started`.
The next real-data action is still TERR-200/TERR-201 under the R2 gate: pin the
official release/terms and acquire only an approved area of interest before
any global production build.
